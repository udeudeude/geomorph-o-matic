# Lantern Sink | research and interpretation | 19 November 2026

## Informed by

1. [National Park Service, Solution Caves](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/caves/solution-caves.htm). Water widens limestone fractures and active streams later incise and scour existing passages. The drowned center, abandoned dry side pockets and silt reflect this sequence, not arbitrary ornamental cave geometry.
2. [British Cave Research Association, Cave Surveying](https://bcra.org.uk/surveying/). Actual plan/profile accuracy requires measured traverses and detailed observations; no survey grade is claimed for this fictional dungeon.
3. [Dyson Logos, Geomorphic Halls Level 4](https://dysonlogos.blog/2019/07/15/geomorphic-4/). A quiet lake and vertical connection can organize exploration within freely swappable geomorphs.
4. [Dyson Logos, Geomorphs Set 6](https://dysonlogos.blog/2019/08/29/geomorphs6/). Natural entrance, cavern pool and partial collapse demonstrate diverse spatial types with interoperable edges.

## Design

A ruined pilgrimage descent is superimposed on a joint-guided limestone cave. Seven unequal, separately authored voids organize the map around the broad sunken pool. A two-cell bridge is genuinely dry where it overlays water. A four-cell raised eastern rock bench has three independently registered grade transitions. The western choke is a blocked dry edge whose alternate path is longer. Two secret discoveries are optional and do not supply the only route between standard exits. The 20-by-20 half-square modular perimeter is exact after every quarter-turn; only the eight designated boundary cells open.

Passage throats are natural unframed openings. Flowstone, scallops, breakdown and gravel are legible but nonblocking geological cues. The print view is a proof, not final commercial artwork.

## Limitations

Roof, ceiling clearance, rock stability, bench slope, plank-span support and groundwater conditions are not surveyed or calculated. Test coverage verifies geometric coherence, floor connectivity, three grade transitions, the collapsed edge, modular seams and deterministic editable vectors, but cannot replace a physical 100-percent print inspection or tabletop playtest.
