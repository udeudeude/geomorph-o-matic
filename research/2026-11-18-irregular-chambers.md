# Irregular geometries: research-to-implementation decision

18 November 2026

Useful references:
- Dyson Logos, *Vault of the Cave Morphs* (2018): https://dysonlogos.blog/2018/06/25/vault-of-the-cave-morphs/
- Dyson Logos, *Random Dungeon Entrances I* (2011): https://dysonlogos.blog/2011/06/02/random-thursday-d8-dungeon-entrance-maps/
- The Alexandrian, *Jaquaying the Dungeon* (2010): https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/13085/roleplaying-games/jaquaying-the-dungeon
- *Tunnel Mapping* symbols and elevation conventions: https://clubhouse5e.com/library/tunnel-mapping/

The first three original studies rely on axis-aligned rectangles for every room. This produces an artificially regular interior. The new *Basalt Rift* has seven chambers built from irregular row runs and two corridor bypasses; the *Sunken Orrery* has a tiered octagonal central court and three secondary bypasses. Each remains exactly interoperable under 90-degree rotation.

These remain **architectural experiments**, not accepted finished map artwork. The underlying 20x20 cell lattice gives stepped rock boundaries, and furniture/symbol readability at conventional print scale requires further inspection. Research suggests testing dedicated cave wall strokes, variable passage width, true stairs/elevation topology, rock faulting, and locked/secret route dependency. Do not copy reference cartography or assets; use it to evaluate original designs.
