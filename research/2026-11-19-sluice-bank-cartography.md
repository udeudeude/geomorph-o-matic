# Riftwater sluice bank and cartographic grid
19 November 2026

## Evidence and design implications

Historic England's [Historic Watermill Landscapes](https://historicengland.org.uk/research/results/reports/7544/HistoricWatermillLandscapesANationalOverview) distinguishes headraces, bypass channels, regulating sluices and tailraces. Our map should not present undifferentiated flooded rooms as a complete hydraulic system.

The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers' [HEC-RAS hydraulic structures reference](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/rasdocs/ras1dtechref/latest/modeling-pipe-networks/pipe-network-hydraulic-structures) distinguishes sluice gates and models water flow according to gate openings. Geomorph-o-matic only validates connectivity and floor thresholds, not hydrodynamic discharge.

[Engineering and technology of industrial water power at Castleford Mills](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4759717/) documents sluice operation from inside the mill through remote shaft and pinion arrangements. The south operating wheel can be plausible, but the linkage is not yet dimensioned.

## Built intervention

The Riftwater Lock now has a three-part stone-and-iron shutter bank on internal edge pairs (9,10) to (9,11), (10,10) to (10,11), and (11,10) to (11,11). Each bay spans one 2.5-foot half-cell and the barrage is 7.5 feet across. In the normal state all three shutters are sealed. In the authored surge, only the center opens, admitting water to three interconnected downstream sump cells. Two flanking shutters stay closed. Tests verify the entire closed barrage blocks this flood.

The normal and surge diagrams now render actual shutter states as separately editable SVG subgroups with identifying attributes. Sluice states never alter ordinary pedestrian navigation; they only affect hydraulic reachability.

This representation establishes a real controllable basin boundary, but does not specify upstream head, gate lift mechanism clearances, overflow channel, water discharge, duration or water volume. We do not claim a complete physically calibrated hydraulic system.

## Cartographic adjustment

At half an inch per five-foot square, the old 2.5-foot construction-grid strokes measured 0.132 mm, below our conservative 0.15 mm print screen. The default vector masters now draw five-foot grid lines only, improving breathing room around locks, hatching and narrow corridors. A complete half-cell drafting grid remains available using the optional half-grid rendering setting.

The audit measures stroke thickness, but cannot establish actual contrast, symbol recognition, or readability without a physical proof.
