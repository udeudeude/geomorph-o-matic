# Sheetflow bench: authored absolute roof envelope (2026-11-20)

## Geological constraints and research
The USGS [*Selected caves and lava-tube systems in and near Lava Beds National Monument* (1990)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b1673), especially Post Office Cave, describes superposed lava passages and surviving lava-flow benches. The published map and profile on plate 5 illustrate why a single headroom number is not a sufficient description of cave architecture. [Kauahikaua et al. (1998)](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/observations-basaltic-lava-streams-tubes-kilauea-volcano-island-hawaii) distinguish tall roofed channels from broad inflated sheet-flow conduits, often only 1 to 2 meters high. [Halliday (1998)](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/ijs/vol27/iss1/11/) documents low nested chambers and partially melted septa in drained sheet flows. These justify considering **uneven vaulting**, not copying survey measurements into our invented cave. The [National Speleological Society's cartography criteria](https://caves.org/salons/cartography-submissions/cartography-judging-criteria/) require a marked vertical datum and agreement between plan and sections.

## Three implemented steps
1. **Resolve the missing physical datum.** Every fictional floor and ceiling sample now has an absolute z value relative to the ordinary-floor entrances. Eight bench floor cells are +1 foot; mouths are z=0 floor under a z=6.5-foot roof. The old local-clearance assumption is no longer mistakenly treated as an absolute crown.
2. **Author a nonuniform shell.** A 7.5-foot central inflated lobe drops to a 7-foot perimeter and 6.5 feet at its eastern tail. Roof height changes are at most 0.5 foot across any adjacent floor cell except deliberately pinched low ceilings elsewhere. At the eastern bench tail, one cell now offers 5.5 feet of headroom and cannot be traversed upright. All original rock, steps, chokes and interface widths remain real and unchanged.
3. **Register drawings and circulation tests.** The editable canonical SVG now contains a separate absolute-vault layer. A newly generated architectural plate and report use actual walking paths over all three real stairs, with equal horizontal and vertical scale. Independent JavaScript and Python checks reconstruct posture movement, ceiling gradients, floor elevations, and reject corrupted source coordinates.

## Critique and remaining work
All elevations are **authored fiction**. A half-foot grade limit is a conservative modeling convention, not a measured lava-tube rule or structural safety limit. The roof is not a true three-dimensional solid, has no surrounding rock thickness model, and no measured transverse sections. The stair geometry remains regular. The longitudinal profiles are unfolded walks, not genuine straight survey cuts. Compare USGS plate 5 cross-sections in a future pass, develop rock-thickness/overburden geometry, and conduct actual-size print inspection and tabletop playtesting before treating these as finished maps.
