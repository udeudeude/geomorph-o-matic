# Entrance-crown transitions and route-unfolded basalt roof profiles
20 November 2026 · geometric design study, not a geological survey

## What the primary evidence actually permits
- Waters, Donnelly-Nolan and Rogers (1990), *Selected Caves and Lava-Tube Systems in and near Lava Beds National Monument*, U.S. Geological Survey Bulletin 1673: https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/b1673 . The Post Office Cave description distinguishes a fairly even oval Cocoa Pipeline (reported about 12–14 feet high and 18–20 feet wide) from a much higher, vertically elongated Silver Connector (reported ceiling heights of 30–45 feet) and records a downstream passage that progressively narrows into a 1–2-foot crawler. Source text: https://npshistory.com/publications/geology/bul/1673/sec3g.htm . The examples support *spatially variable headroom*, not a claim that all basalt cavities taper identically.
- Geological Society of America, *Analysis of a Lava Tube with Lidar on Mauna Loa Volcano, Hawai‘i* (2017): https://gsa.confex.com/gsa/2017CD/webprogram/Paper292954.html . Its surveyed 180-meter portion spans 0.8–10.2 meters in width and 2.3–6 meters in height, with local roof thickness of 1.4–3.6 meters. A real roof and floor cannot be extrapolated from an overhead plan.
- *Resolution of Lava Tubes With Ground Penetrating Radar: The TubeX Project* (2020): https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1029/2019je006138 . Radar and laser scans of Skull and Valentine Caves provide distinct void geometries; complex three-dimensional geometry can compromise two-dimensional recovery. This is particularly relevant to our multi-level and constricted fictional cavities.

None of these sources measures our invented caves, endorses 6.5-foot mouths, or gives a universal 0.5-foot rise over 2.5 feet. The number is an **explicit illustrative design constraint** for smoothing one formerly discontinuous visual assumption, not a geological constant, structural guarantee, or accessibility standard.

## Three architectural decisions
1. **Replace arbitrary ceiling jumps at the interface.** The first two 2.5-foot inward floor stations of every five-foot-wide modular entrance retain 6.5 feet of assumed *local* clear height. In Braided Lava and Split-Spine, each successive occupied-floor grid station may increase by at most 0.5 feet, ending at the respective 8- or 7.5-foot local vault assumption. Sagging Sheetflow's 6.5-foot interior requires no upward taper. The original genuine low-ceiling pinch zones remain separate and may change height abruptly at their edges. Distances propagate only through occupied adjacent plan cells, not through the enclosing rock.
2. **Register overhead contour bands to the same source geometry as passages.** Editable vector maps now show understated dashed crown-change interfaces, separately from walls, roof collapses, steps, water and the low-ceiling zone annotations. These additional symbols represent a fictional clearance *hypothesis*, not solid barriers. The existing circulation graph and 5-foot modular width are unchanged.
3. **Unfold a genuine traversable route to each low-ceiling pinch.** The new report and vector plate follow dry walking edges from the north mouth to an authored roof constriction, preserving the actual 2.5-foot travel increment. Horizontal and local-clearance vertical scales in each plotted profile are identical. Because routes bend, their displayed plan stations are *unrolled along the path*, not an undistorted projected geological cross section. The selected routes stay on floor level zero; the raised Sheetflow bench is not silently assigned an absolute roof elevation.

## Reproduce and independently challenge
Run `node wide-roof-transitions.js`, `node --test tests/wide-roof-transitions.test.js`, and `PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_wide_roof_transitions.py'`. The full workflow also reruns every JavaScript and Python terrain contract and writes `reports/wide-roof-transitions.json` and `studies/wide_roof_transitions.svg`.

The JavaScript validator checks actual route edges, contour-rate bounds, protected mouth cells and location of genuine constrictions. An independent Python reconstruction derives the distance field from raw authored floor occupancy and asserts that every non-pinch adjacent pair differs by at most 0.5 feet.

## Remaining engineering objections
- Geodesic distance across a grid of occupied plan cells produces a tidy numerical taper. Natural lava crusts can be much less regular, and some cave roof constrictions are abrupt. Future roof profiles should be interpreted and authored region by region, not merely inferred from distance to a seam.
- Existing constrictions still jump abruptly from their adjoining vaults. Their edge positions, profiles, actual rock cover and roof stability remain unknown.
- The prior raised Sheetflow bench is one foot above the ordinary design floor, but the roof model remains *local clearance*, not a reconciled absolute ceiling-elevation field. This new model must not be treated as solving that datum problem.
- Real cave geometry requires measured plan and cross-sections, rock continuity and roof thickness, gradient surveys, print-size review and human exploration testing. Those remain outstanding. No part of this pass makes the dungeon collection release-ready.
