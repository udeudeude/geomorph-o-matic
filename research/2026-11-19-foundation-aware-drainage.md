# 19 November 2026 | A foundation-aware buried drainage route

**Status:** A corrected plan-coordinate route with independent validation, *not* a structurally certified waterwork. These are unfinished cartographic studies.

## Research and problem discovered

The [National Park Service's Harpers Ferry tailrace survey](https://www.nps.gov/articles/harpers-ferry-tailrace.htm) documents arched shale tunnels beneath historic armory buildings, curved connections, and different construction phases. This demonstrates that **crossing beneath a building can be legitimate when the building and tunnel are structurally designed together**. Our preceding plan simply put a lower centerline under the Riftwater pressure vault without representing that structure, its footing depth, or any clearance. The result could wrongly suggest that the vault/tunnel coexistence had been engineered.

We now reserve chamber footprints as conservative buried-route exclusion areas. The sole exception is **three intentional turbine drain penetrations**. This is plan topology only: a room footprint is *not* an actual geotechnical foundation survey, nor proof that adjacent bedrock has adequate bearing.

The [EN 752:2017 principles on access points](https://studylib.es/doc/9184544/bsi-standards-publication-drain-and-sewe) recommend inspection access around changes in buried drainage alignment. The new dogleg needs its own properly located inspection or cleanout, not a decorative hatch. This is a design reference only, not a claim that historic construction follows current sewer standards.

## Three completed changes

1. **Riftwater route:** The existing separately controlled pickups at (9,11), (10,11), (11,11) feed a lower run south to (11,14), then west to (6,14), then south to (6,19). The lower route no longer crosses the pressure-vault *plan footprint*. Assumed invert -10.50 to -11.25 feet over 37.5 feet.
2. **Postern route:** It now receives at (6,0), goes to (6,1), east to (9,1), then south to (9,8). This bypasses the toll and barracks chamber footprints. Assumed invert -11.30 to -11.85 feet over 27.5 feet. The matched seam still descends 0.05 feet. Combined authored plan length, including a 2.5-foot seam station, is **67.5 feet**; hydraulic capacity is not calculated.
3. **Engineering checks and editable drawing:** The cross-language room-footprint audit detects unintended buried overlap and identifies stations one half-cell from a modeled chamber as unresolved proximity warnings. SVG technical plates now trace the revised physical route and use *actual route lengths* when placing profile seam indices, rather than hardcoded eleven-station assumptions. All upper surface portals remain unchanged.

This is an architectural correction, not proof that a 2.5-foot centerline grid is a tunnel cross-section. Adjacent footings, ventilation, pressure loads, hydraulic capacity, cleanout requirements, stone arch spans, and tailwater remain unresolved. Orange caution rings flag modeled adjacency, **not** actual clearances.
