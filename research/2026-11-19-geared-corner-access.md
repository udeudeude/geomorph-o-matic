# Sluice transmission correction: right-angle gears and service recesses

**November 19, 2026 | Schematic engineering study, not working machinery.**

## Found architectural error

Three handwheel-to-shutter shaft paths had five 90-degree turns drawn as uninterrupted rigid shafts. Ordinary shaft bearings do not redirect rotation. Each corner needs a gear train or other explicitly constructed transmission; a thin rock bore is not an adequate gearbox chamber.

## Evidence

1. [American Society of Mechanical Engineers, Boyden hydraulic turbines](https://www.asme.org/about-asme/engineering-history/landmarks/5-boyden-hydraulic-turbines) documents transfer from vertical turbine shafts to horizontal overhead shafts via bevel gearing. This establishes a historical mechanical principle, not this project's gearing dimensions, forces or age.
2. [National Park Service, Lowell mill power drives](https://www.nps.gov/articles/lowell-handbook-mill-power-drives.htm) documents geared vertical/horizontal line shafts and their historic problems with noise, imprecise machining and breakdowns, eventually motivating belting.
3. [Historic England, Tealby Watermill](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1165464) records pit-wheel and bevel-gear transmission into a vertical shaft.
4. [National Park Service, Harpers Ferry tailrace](https://www.nps.gov/articles/harpers-ferry-tailrace.htm) documents a separate arched underground race, reinforcing the distinction between dry mechanical equipment and submerged lower conduits.

## Actual changes

1. Every transmission corner is now an authored right-angle bevel gearbox, not an impossible unbroken rigid shaft: west 1, center 2, east 2.
2. Each box has a **tentative** 1.5-foot-wide recess in a 2.5-foot planning cell, plus an overhead sealed access panel facing an actual dry hall position. Boxes reachable from their own handwheels explicitly require shutdown. These are **nominal plan allocations**, not verified machinery envelopes.
3. The engineering plate now separates gear housings, wall-facing service panels, shaft runs, bearings and masonry bores. Independent Python and JavaScript validators reject unmarked corners, inappropriate gearbox locations, missing service access and unsafe control interference. The gearbox risk report calls out rock cuts, shared service landings and proximity to the wet race.

## Remaining serious limitations

No gear ratios, rotation direction, self-locking brakes, gate loading, torque, shaft deflection, bearing reactions, oiling provision, ventilation or structural anchoring have been calculated. The two adjacent panels accessing from a single 2.5-foot landing may be impractical. Height at +8 feet requires engineered means of reaching the panels, not just a symbol in the plan. Floodproofing rock recesses remains hypothetical.

The receiving water elevation and **downstream outfall remain unmapped**. Neither this research nor automated tests establish construction feasibility or finished dungeon artwork.
