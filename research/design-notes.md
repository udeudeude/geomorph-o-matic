# Research basis and next investigations

- [Dyson Logos, Geomorphic Halls Level 3](https://dysonlogos.blog/2019/06/17/geomorphic-3/): cramped and spacious areas and identifiable landmarks in composite maps.
- [Dyson Logos, Geomorph #38](https://dysonlogos.blog/2010/03/02/geomorph-mapping-challenge-38/): balconies and vertical relationships that need not be connected on a single tile.
- [Dyson Logos, Cave Morphs](https://dysonlogos.blog/2018/06/25/vault-of-the-cave-morphs/): naturalistic passages and purpose-designed interface widths.
- [Dyson Logos, Dead Ends](https://dysonlogos.blog/2010/10/01/friday-map-geomorph-set-17-dead-ends/): special tiles are a legitimate complement to full universal morphs.
- [Dungeon and Spatial Topology](https://pvliesdonk.github.io/gamebook/content/articles/space-props-materiality/dungeon-and-spatial-topology.html): measure real path loops, not spurious interior-room cell cycles.

The initial three layouts are entirely original, not traced reproductions. Architectural credibility is still the limiting factor, not the connection engine.

**Research next:** Study cave/irregular polygon semantics, sample physical print scale of door and stair symbols, examine vertical and secret-route conventions, and measure how many alternate paths a party actually perceives in an assembled map.
