# Cave rock webs and absolute walking profiles
November 20, 2026. Unreleased geological and architectural study.

## Primary research and actual consequences for the design

1. Waters, Donnelly-Nolan and Rogers, *Selected Caves and Lava-Tube Systems in and near Lava Beds National Monument*, USGS Bulletin 1673 (1990), Post Office Cave: https://npshistory.com/publications/geology/bul/1673/sec3g.htm . Its plan and longitudinal section (map 15, plate 5) describe vertically superposed conduits and collapse connections. The Cocoa Pipeline is broadly oval; the Silver Connector is vertically elongated. Cross-sectional form cannot be inferred from footprint alone.
2. The same USGS Bulletin, Skull Cave: https://npshistory.com/publications/geology/bul/1673/sec3d.htm . Roof breakdown and collapsed upper floors alter actual cave routes. A route profile must not pass straight through rock.
3. National Park Service, lava caves and tubes: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/caves/lava-caves-or-tubes.htm . Lava tubes are often shallow, while others have been buried under newer flows. Underground ceiling elevation does not determine overburden thickness.
4. *Modal Analysis of a Lava Tube Roof Complex: Tabernacle Hill, Utah, USA*, Rock Mechanics and Rock Engineering (2024): https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00603-024-03868-9 . Fractures and discontinuities influence roof response. Simple horizontal strip width and assumed roof cover DO NOT establish stability.

## Three purposeful changes and their limits

**Research first.** The sources indicate that a coherent dungeon needs attention to stacked tubes, collapse, fragile webs and variable floor/ceiling geometry. The current plans are not surveyed and should not pretend to be.

**Geological rock-web integrity check.** A new independent audit flags one-half-cell (2.5-foot) rock septa with real walkable floor on both sides. Rather than pretending to calculate bearing capacity, it measures an actual dry walking bypass for each thin partition, including the authored closures and steps. A rock web that separates two floor cells whose alternate route is long warrants an architectural review. Single rock cells at local bays need not be important. This is prioritization, NOT a failure threshold or a hazard claim. The master walls stay unchanged pending deliberate redesign.

**Absolute-datum architectural sections along real traversable routes.** Three newly registered and editable SVG drawings follow actual occupied floor from the north modular mouth to named roof features. They plot independent floor and ceiling elevations. Sheetflow's eastern bench route must cross genuine modeled steps, reach a one-foot rise, and end at its explicitly fictional 5.5-foot stoop. Braided Lava targets the nine-foot confluence crest; Split-Spine targets the south drainback arch. Their passages turn, so the section axis is UNFOLDED WALKING DISTANCE, not an invented straight geological cross section. Each profile includes a highlighted plan inset.

Generate the new vector files and machine-readable reports by running:
- node wide-rock-webs.js: studies/wide_rock_webs.svg and reports/wide-rock-webs.json.
- node wide-absolute-walking-profiles.js: studies/wide_absolute_walking_profiles.svg and reports/wide-absolute-walking-profiles.json.

The JavaScript regression suite checks that candidate webs are real rock, both banks are occupied, routes use genuine traversable edges, roof and floor z are separate, and all drawn stairs are actual access edges. Continuous integration regenerates and preserves the vector files.
